Edit the entries on the left. They are compiled in your browser with `@shipdot/core/compile`, the
same parser and sanitizer that `shipdot build` uses, and fed to the `<ship-dot>` widget in the demo
app. Nothing leaves your browser: your edits live in the page address (after the `#`), so a reload
keeps them and **Copy link** shares them.
